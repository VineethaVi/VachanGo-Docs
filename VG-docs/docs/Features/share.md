# Share
Users can share the chapter and verse with their friends and family by clicking the share icon. To access the share feature, the user must click the share icon **(1)**.
There are two choices to share:
1. Click "Copy to Clipboard" **(2)** to copy the URL and share it across various platforms.
2. Share directly on social media platforms like WhatsApp, Gmail, and LinkedIn **(3)**.
